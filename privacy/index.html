Privacy Policy

PropSize · Last updated: 4 October 2026

PropSize is a position size calculator for prop traders. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable law including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR.

1. Who we are

PropSize is operated by Justice Ughelie, based in Ireland. You can contact us at hello@propsize.app.

2. What data we collect

PropSize is designed to work with minimal data collection.

Your calculator settings: Your firm selection, configured account size, risk preferences, instrument settings and broker overrides are saved on your device. If you allow app-usage sharing, selected setup values and calculation outcomes are also sent to PostHog as described below. PropSize does not connect to your trading account or read your trading history.

Subscriptions: Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat processes subscription identifiers, product identifiers, transaction records, trial and renewal dates, purchase environment and entitlement status to manage access and restores. PropSize does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. RevenueCat also sends subscription lifecycle records, including a subscriber identifier and transaction information, to PostHog for purchase reconciliation. These records are processed separately from optional app-usage sharing. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Optional app-usage sharing: In version 1.1.2 with the privacy controls, app-usage sharing is off until you allow it. You can change your choice in Settings > Share app usage. When enabled, PostHog receives screen and interaction events, structured setup choices such as firm, configured account size and currency, calculation outcomes such as instrument, lot size and risk percentage, session counters, timing and checkout outcome codes. Events use a persistent app identifier that may be linked to a RevenueCat subscriber identifier. This is pseudonymous data, not completely anonymous data. We do not send names, email addresses, typed broker names, trade entry or stop prices, voice recordings or transcripts in these events. Our PostHog project uses EU Cloud. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Reliability diagnostics: Production builds use Sentry for error reports and sampled performance traces. Reports can contain error messages, stack traces, diagnostic breadcrumbs, app and device information, and technical identifiers. Default personal-information collection is disabled in the app configuration; that does not make all technical reports anonymous. These diagnostics are separate from the app-usage sharing control. The configured Sentry ingestion endpoint is in the EU. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Voice input: If you allow microphone and speech access, Apple's speech recognition converts your spoken trade inputs into text. Audio may be processed by Apple; PropSize does not guarantee on-device-only recognition. PropSize does not send voice recordings or transcripts to PostHog. You can turn microphone and speech access off in iOS Settings and use typed inputs instead.

Exchange rates: PropSize requests reference exchange rates from Frankfurter and caches them on your device. Reference rates are not live trade-execution quotes. Requests do not include your configured account size or trade inputs, but the service receives ordinary network information such as your IP address.

3. What data we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, broker login or Apple Account password. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We do not sell data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

We process subscription access and restore records to perform our contract with you, and retain records where required by law. Optional app-usage sharing is based on your consent. You can withdraw that consent in the app; this stops new app-usage events and clears unsent events, but does not erase records already received by a provider. We use reliability diagnostics and subscription reconciliation for our legitimate interests in maintaining a working service and investigating faults. You may object to that processing by contacting us. iOS separately asks for microphone and speech permissions.

5. Data retention

Local settings remain until removed from the device. Server records are retained according to their purpose and the provider's applicable retention settings: subscription records for access, restores and required recordkeeping; app-usage records for product analysis; and diagnostics for fault investigation. Deleting the app does not automatically delete server records. Contact us to request deletion or details of the retention settings that apply to your records.

6. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, withdraw consent or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact hello@propsize.app. Do not send passwords or payment details. We may need a subscription or app identifier to locate pseudonymous records and will explain what is needed. You can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission or your local supervisory authority.

7. International transfers

RevenueCat and other providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. EU hosting does not by itself rule out international access or transfers. Each provider's privacy policy and applicable data-processing terms describe its transfer safeguards.

We require third-party providers receiving app data to provide the same or equal protection described in this policy and required by Apple's App Review Guidelines, under applicable data-processing terms.

8. Children

PropSize is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version is available here. Reading this policy or continuing to use the app is not consent to optional app-usage sharing.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@propsize.app.
